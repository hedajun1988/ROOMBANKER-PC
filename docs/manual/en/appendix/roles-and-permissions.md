# Roles and permissions

Access is role-based. The six role families are platform super administrator, platform administrator, platform operator, company owner, company administrator, and employee/normal user.

The current modules are Dashboard, Companies, Hubs, Alarm Messages, Device Messages, PC Operation Log, Accounts, Permissions, Deactivated Accounts, Notifications, and Announcements.

| Role family | Menu and action visibility |
|---|---|
| Platform super administrator | All modules; only role that can save permission-tree changes. |
| Platform administrator | Granted operational and administration modules; permission tree remains read-only. |
| Platform operator | Only assigned operational modules and actions. |
| Company owner | Company-scope company and assigned Hub workflows. |
| Company administrator | Company-scope staff and assigned Hub workflows. |
| Employee/normal user | Explicitly granted personal or assigned workflows only. |

The sidebar and buttons are hidden when an account lacks permission. Typing a page address directly cannot bypass permissions; an unauthorized account returns to an available page.
